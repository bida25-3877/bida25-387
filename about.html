About Us

Our brand story

Luxe Nail Studio was built in the heart of Gaborone Gamecity Mall, starting from the bottom with no customers. It was created with a passion for beauty, creativity and self-expression. We believe that nails are more than just a service — they are a form of art and confidence. Our goal is to create a relaxing space where clients enjoy high-quality nail care while expressing their personal style. From classic manicures to modern gel and acrylic designs, we focus on hygiene, precision and customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality nail care services in a clean, relaxing and welcoming environment. We aim to enhance confidence through creativity, professionalism and excellent customer service.
